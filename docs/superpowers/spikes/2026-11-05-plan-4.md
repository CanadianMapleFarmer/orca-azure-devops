## S2 Log tail cost

P is the number of code projects. Every figure is a count of calls; nothing was sent.

| Question | Method | Result | Decision |
|----------|--------|--------|----------|

In steady state that is at most `4P + 8` calls: for P = 2, 6 lists + 2 batches + 8 timelines = 16 per 30 s (32 a minute). A cold or busy load also reads the timelines of up to 8 newly finished runs for their chips (cached by `buildId` and `finishTime`, LRU 100), so it is at most `4P + 16` calls: 24 for P = 2.
